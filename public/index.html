The MeetUp

Diwali MeetUp brings friends, families, and neighbours together for a thoughtful celebration of light, kindness, and connection.

Come with an open heart, share your festive wishes, and help create a calm and joyful gathering for everyone.

MeetUp Guidelines

Be kind, be respectful, and make space for every guest.

Celebrate responsibly and follow the guidance of the event hosts.

Keep conversations welcoming and help make the gathering comfortable for all.

Terms and Conditions

By taking part in Diwali MeetUp, you agree to treat fellow guests, hosts, and the venue with respect.

Hosts may ask anyone who disrupts the safety, comfort, or inclusive spirit of the gathering to leave.

Event details may change when needed to keep the MeetUp safe and enjoyable. Attendance means accepting these terms.
